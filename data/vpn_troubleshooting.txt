Title: VPN Troubleshooting Guide

If the VPN is connected but an internal application cannot be accessed:
1. Verify that the VPN client reports a successful connection.
2. Disconnect and reconnect to the VPN.
3. Check whether other internal applications are accessible.
4. Verify that the correct internal application URL is being used.
5. If only one application is inaccessible, contact the application support team.
6. If all internal applications are inaccessible, contact the network support team.
